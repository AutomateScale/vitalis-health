### 2.2 Meta Ad Library Messaging Analysis & Dallas Ad Hooks
A sweep of active Meta Ad Library campaigns running in Dallas (Radius: Dallas + 25 miles) reveals four dominant messaging patterns:

1. **The "Hangover Rescue" Meme Angle:** Low-production memes or generic video clips showing hungover individuals waking up on couches. *Conversion reality:* High CTR, but low AOV ($120), high cancellation rates, and zero client lifetime value.
2. **The "Discount Voucher" Angle:** "$25 Off Your First Drip" or "Buy 1 Get 1 50% Off." *Conversion reality:* Attracts deal-seekers who churn immediately and resist high-ticket protocols like NAD+ ($900/mo) or The Reserve ($500).
3. **The "Cold/Flu Urgent Care" Angle:** "Down with the flu? Skip the ER waiting room." *Conversion reality:* Highly seasonal, low repeat rate, difficult to scale consistently in summer months.
4. **The Retail Footprint Push (Prime IV / Restore):** "Visit our Mockingbird location today." *Conversion reality:* Leaves an enormous white space for high-net-worth individuals who value time above all else and refuse to drive to a strip mall.
