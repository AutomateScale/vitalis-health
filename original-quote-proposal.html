What we're building

One system, not a pile of tactics.

Every traffic source — paid ads, your organic content, the Substack list, the gym partnership — points at the same place: a booking. Behind that booking sits a CRM that knows who came from where, who booked, who didn't, and what to say to each of them next.

The single highest-leverage mechanic in this category is speed to lead. A booking gets a call within five minutes to confirm it. That one behavior moves show rate more than any creative decision we'll make. It is also the part that depends on you, and we've flagged it as a decision below.

The second is the follow-up. An IV client isn't a one-time transaction — they're a subscription that hasn't been asked for yet. Rebooking, referrals, reviews, and the NAD+ ascension all get built in Phase 02, not bolted on later.
